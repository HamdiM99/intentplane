## intentplane 0.7.0: confirm outside the client, and a real ERP

**Confirming a step-up in Claude Desktop.** Clients without MCP elicitation (Claude Desktop today) could not
confirm a step-up, so the operation was refused. With `[approvals]` in the proxy config, the agent relays a link
to a local approval page: the user signs in with a passcode, sees the resolved operation (the trusted payee, the
amount, every other argument and where each critical value comes from) and approves it; the agent calls the tool
again and it executes.

- The approval is signed over that exact operation, single use, and expires with its request.
- The page refuses to show anything that does not hash to the operation being approved; any change (one cent,
  another payee, another subject) is another operation with its own approval.
- Without the passcode, nothing can be approved and no request can be created. Passcodes are stored as scrypt
  hashes (`python -m intentplane.approvals hash-passcode`); CSRF tokens, lockout after repeated failures.
- Requests and decisions are hash-chained receipts.
- `ApprovalChannel`: a documented interface for other channels (more approvers, four eyes). Four eyes without
  such a channel is refused.

**A real ERP.** The accounts-payable pack reads its systems of record through an `ERPSource` interface, with an
Odoo connector (`intentplane.connectors.odoo`, three separate identities: read-only, payer, bank administrator)
and an end-to-end demo on Odoo 17 in Docker (`examples/odoo/`).
